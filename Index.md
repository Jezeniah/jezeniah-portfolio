## **BodyLink — AI‑Assisted Personal Health Record & Insight System**

BodyLink is a comprehensive health management application that gives users a centralized, intelligent view of their entire medical history. It blends document storage, body‑map navigation, AI‑generated summaries, and trend tracking into one unified experience.

**Core Features**
- Full medical record storage (lab reports, diagnostics, visit summaries)  
- Interactive body map for navigating health issues by region  
- AI‑generated non‑diagnostic summaries of uploaded reports  
- Doctor directory with appointment history and linked records  
- Pulse and blood pressure tracking  
- Trend detection across visits, labs, and vitals  
- Exportable health records  

**Impact**  
BodyLink demonstrates the ability to build a complex, multi‑layered health application that organizes medical data responsibly and intelligently.

## **Peak Flow — AI‑Powered Fitness & Nutrition App**

Peak Flow is a full‑featured fitness application that helps users track workouts, monitor weight changes, and receive personalized AI‑generated plans for meals and exercise.

**Core Features**
- User accounts and profiles  
- Exercise tracking with calorie output  
- Weight logging with trend graphs  
- AI‑generated meal plans (daily, weekly, monthly)  
- AI‑generated workout plans (core, arms, legs, full body)  
- AI insights based on age, weight, and activity  
- Dashboard with upcoming meals, workouts, and calorie output  
- Meal and exercise library (user‑created + AI‑generated)  
- CSV export for logs  

**Impact**  
Peak Flow blends fitness tracking with AI‑driven planning to support users on weight‑loss or strength‑building journeys.
